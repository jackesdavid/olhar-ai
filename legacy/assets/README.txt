Drop your sound files here (names are exact):

  boot_loop.wav   long looping "blinks and noise" hum during boot
  beep.wav        short blip, fires as each boot line prints
  ready.wav       chime when INTERFACE 2037 comes online

OGG also works (the build enables the vorbis decoder).

Sourcing (license-clean — do NOT use the original film audio in anything you share):
  - Pixabay  -> "boot sequence" sound effects, royalty-free, no attribution
  - itch.io  -> free retro SFX packs (WAV/OGG)
  - 8-Bit Beep Box (itch.io) -> generate your own beeps, export WAV
  - Pro Sound Effects -> a paid "Nostromo" retro-computer telemetry clip (closest licensed match)

If this folder is empty or there's no audio device, the app just runs silently.
