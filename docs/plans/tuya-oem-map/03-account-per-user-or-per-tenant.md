# Is the invisible Tuya account per user, or per tenant?

Type: grilling
Status: open

## Question

The account is invisible — decided. What it is attached to is not, and this is the most structural
question on the map.

**Per user.** Each person's devices live in their own Tuya account. Clean identity, and it breaks
the product: a tenant is a shared space, and today every user of a tenant sees the same devices. Two
people in one house would each pair their own plugs and neither would see the other's.

**Per tenant.** One Tuya account behind the tenant, everyone pairs into it. Matches how the product
already behaves — but then who owns it? What happens when the person who created it leaves, or is
removed? An account nobody is attached to still holds every device in the house.

The consequences to work through either way, because "invisible" makes them ours to handle:

- Password reset and email change on the Olhar AI side — does the Tuya side need to follow?
- **Account deletion.** A user asking us to delete their data now implies deleting an account at a
  third party. Under LGPD that is not optional, and it must be possible without a support ticket.
- The same person on two phones.
- A tenant with several users pairing at once.

Resolve with: what does a two-person household expect to see, and what happens to the devices when
one of them leaves.
